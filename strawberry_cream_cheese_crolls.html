Strawberry Cream Cheese Crescent Rolls

Description

I was craving Perkin's strawberry cream cheese appetizer pastry things, so I made this up to fix the craving. First tried 8/13/2026.


Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb strawberries
  • lemon/lemon juice
  • milk
  • butter
  • salt
  • granular sugar
  • whipped cream cheese topping (for bagels)
  • a package of crescent rolls (or three)

Steps

  1. chop the strawberries up at least in halves
  2. wam the butter up on medium heat and sautee the strawberries with a splash of the milk
  3. after the strawberries have sauteed awhile, you can mash them down into a chunky paste by smashing them down with a fork
  4. add in some sugar. Measure with distain for your own health.
  5. add the pinch of a salt and some lemon juice (at least a couple drops, but to taste)
  6. cook down to desired tecture, you might need some more milk if it's too dry. It should be like a syrup with strawberry pieces.
  7. set off to the side to cool
  8. open up your crescent rolls and set the oven to preheat.
  9. put the whipped cream cheese topping onto the crescent rolls first
  10. then add the strawberry sauce
  11. roll them up and bake
